## Topic

**Impact of Social Media on Students' Academic Performance**

## Problem Statement

Does social media affect students' academic performance?

## Evidence

* Students spend 3–5 hours daily on social media.
* Excessive use reduces study time.
* Educational content on social media can improve learning.
* Research articles show both positive and negative impacts.

## Assumptions

* Students have equal access to the internet.
* Academic performance depends on study habits.
* Time management influences learning outcomes.

## Inferences

* High social media usage may decrease grades.
* Educational use of social media may improve knowledge.
* Balanced usage can lead to better academic outcomes.

## Conclusion

Social media has both positive and negative effects on students. The overall impact depends on how students use social media and manage their time.
